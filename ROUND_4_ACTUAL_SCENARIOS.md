# Round 4 — When There Is No Easy Christian Answer

**12 actual scenario nodes.**

This round moves into competing goods and real trade-offs: caring for parents without destabilising the household; relocation and career opportunity; opposite-sex emotional boundaries; caregiving and career sacrifice; business loans and faith; pregnancy and medical urgency; schooling and Christian formation; public disagreement; wealth and extended-family requests; spiritual growth and changing convictions; chronic illness and role reversal; and serious value conflicts where love does not automatically create agreement.

The design principle is **wisdom under tension**. No option is rewarded merely because it sounds religious, cautious, generous or independent. The engine looks for transparency, informed consent, humility, responsibility, dignity, evidence, shared decision-making and the ability to revisit a plan.

Each node includes transformation metadata: hidden tension, self-deception trap, evidence to watch, mirror question, ten-year lens and transformation move.
