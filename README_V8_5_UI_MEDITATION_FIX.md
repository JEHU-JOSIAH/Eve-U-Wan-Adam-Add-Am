## V8.5 Meditation Source Update — Waiting and Dating

The Meditation library now includes source-grounded themes from Myles Munroe's *Waiting and Dating: A Sensible Guide to a Fulfilling Love*, including:
- dating readiness and the dangers of premature emotional/physical involvement;
- learning to be whole and purposeful while single;
- seeking God's Kingdom rather than making romance the center of identity;
- walking in spiritual agreement;
- the progression of spirit, soul, and body;
- friendship as a foundation for long-term relationship;
- intimacy as something that develops through trust and time;
- asking questions and listening to understand;
- discerning contribution, gifts, goals, and life direction;
- engagement as practical preparation for marriage.

The app uses short attributed excerpts and original reflections rather than reproducing the book. The uploaded edition identifies itself as copyright 2004 by Myles Munroe and notes that short quotations or occasional page copying for personal/group study are permitted. The source file is kept outside the app bundle; only the curated meditation data is included.
