# Round 3 — When Your Own Answers Come Back to You

**12 revelation-stage scenarios.** This round intentionally refers back to principles and commitments established in earlier rounds. It tests whether a player's values remain stable when the same issue returns from the opposite direction.

Themes: legitimate family warnings vs prejudice; income/power reversal; spiritual claims and discernment; forgiveness vs accountability; family generosity vs household stewardship; prayer and responsible medical care; calling vs relational cost; attraction and social comparison; parenting values under exhaustion; status and jealousy; sunk cost and selective evidence; future change and the illusion of fixing a partner.
