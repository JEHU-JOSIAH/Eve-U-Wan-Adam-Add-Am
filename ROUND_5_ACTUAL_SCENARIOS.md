# Round 5 — When Character Costs You Something

12 actual scenario nodes. This round tests whether values survive when honesty, boundaries, humility, stewardship and commitment become costly.

Themes include integrity when reputation is at stake; repeated promises and the “marriage will change them” illusion; power after promotion; family secrecy; apology versus changed behaviour; financial help to relatives; ministry boundaries; attraction through pregnancy and changing seasons; parenting faith and conscience; success and resentment; long-term caregiving; and final evidence-over-fantasy discernment.

The round is designed to reveal patterns rather than pronounce a verdict. A player may continue, pause, seek counsel, work on himself or herself, or walk away depending on the full journey and evidence.
