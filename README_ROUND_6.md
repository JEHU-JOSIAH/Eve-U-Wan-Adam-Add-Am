# Eve U Wan Adam Add Am — Round 6

## Covenant Under Pressure

Round 6 adds 12 high-stakes relationship scenarios designed to test how beliefs behave when trust, money, health, sexuality, leadership, fertility, family conflict, ministry, moral failure and long-term commitment are under pressure.

The round is cumulative: previous rounds remain part of the relationship memory, scores and pattern history. The game should not treat one answer as a diagnosis or automatic marriage verdict.

### Round-end controls
- Show Round GPA
- Show Round Percentage
- Show Letter Grade
- Show Cumulative GPA
- Show category performance and patterns
- **Continue to Next Round**
- **End Journey & View Final Report**

Continuing carries previous scores, decisions, memories, patterns, consequences and scenario state into the next round.
